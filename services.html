Our Services

What We Do For Our Clients

From new websites to digital advice and support, we provide the practical help small businesses and organizations need.

Get in touch

  • Web Design

    We create custom designs that focus on functionality first. Our designs support your business goals and make it easy for visitors to find the information they need.

  • Who its for:

    Small businesses and organizations that want a website that reflects who they are and what they do without compromising on usability.

    Key Benefits:

    • Intuitive layouts that guide users to what they need
    • Responsive designs for mobile, tablet, and desktop
    • Accurate brand representation

  • Web Development

    We hand-code every static website we build to ensure optimal performance, security, and long-term stability. Our approach keeps things fast, simple, and easy to manage.

  • Who its for:

    Businesses and organizations that want a reliable, low-maintenance website without the overhead of complex platforms.

    Key Benefits:

    • Accessibility and on-page SEO best practices
    • Clean, semantic code tailored to your goals
    • Fast load times and improved security

  • Digital Consultation

    We help you choose and set up the right digital tools for your business. No jargon, just clear guidance that supports your goals.

  • Who its for:

    Small businesses and organizations that need help with tools like e-commerce platforms, booking systems, newsletters, online courses, and more.

    Key Benefits:

    • Help selecting and setting up the right tools
    • Clear, tailored, jargon-free advice
    • Confidence in your digital setup

  • WordPress Support

    We offer reliable WordPress support. From updates and fixes to full redesigns and ongoing maintenance, we’ve got you covered.

  • Who its for:

    Businesses and organizations already using WordPress who need support, design improvements, or troubleshooting—without hiring a full-time developer.

    Key Benefits:

    • Support for full redesigns or migrations
    • Fixes and layout tweaks when needed
    • Maintenance, updates, and backups
